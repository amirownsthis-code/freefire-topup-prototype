Finding Your Free Fire Player ID (UID)

Follow these quick steps to locate your unique 9 to 10 digit Player ID inside Free Fire:

  • 1. Launch the Free Fire app on your mobile device.
  • 2. Click on your profile banner in the top-left corner of the main lobby screen.
  • 3. Look directly under your in-game nickname to find your Player ID (UID).
  • 4. Click the small copy icon next to your ID to copy it to your clipboard.

Payment Troubleshooting

If your account balance was deducted but diamonds have not appeared, check your SMS inbox for confirmation. If unresolved, contact Garena Customer Care with your transaction ID.
