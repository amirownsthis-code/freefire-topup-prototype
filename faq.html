Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I top up Free Fire Diamonds on Garena Official Top Up Center?

Enter your Free Fire Player ID into the login box, select your preferred diamond package or membership deal, choose your payment method (EasyPaisa, JazzCash, Zong, Telenor, Konnect), and click "Buy Now" to finalize payment.

2. How long does it take for diamonds to be credited to my account?

Transactions are processed instantly. In rare cases of network delay, please wait up to 15 minutes before contacting support.

3. What should I do if I entered the wrong Player ID?

Top-ups are non-refundable once delivered. Always verify your Player ID nickname carefully before proceeding with payment.

4. Which payment channels are available in Pakistan?

We support EasyPaisa, JazzCash, Jazz Mobile Billing, Telenor Mobile Billing, Zong DCB, and Konnect by HBL.

5. How do I redeem a Garena Card?

Switch to the "Redeem" tab on the homepage, log in with your Player ID, choose your card denomination, and enter your Garena card PIN.
